The game

Meet Happy Fish 🐠

Happy Fish is a browser arcade game. You are a small fish in a busy reef: eat the fish smaller than you, run from the bigger ones and grow into the king of the ocean.

🌐 Plays in any browser📱 Phone and computer💾 Cloud saves🔊 With sound

More details
Smart tricks inside the code

  

    
🔊
Web Audio
Every sound and the home song are synthesised with oscillators. No audio files.

    
📷
Finger counting
Skin-colour mask, palm centre from a distance transform, then fingers counted as arcs.

    
🎤
Clap counting
The microphone level is watched for sharp peaks, one per clap.

    
💰
Continue
After a defeat, pay 500 coins or 10 gems to restart the same stage.
